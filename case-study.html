01 Overview

A clearer way to track an order.

Delivery App UI/UX design project: from problem definition and user flow to wireframes, high-fidelity screens and an interactive Figma prototype. Tool: Figma.

02 Problem

Users don't know when their order will arrive.

Delivery is often delayed and users do not have a clear understanding of when their order will arrive. Identified user pain points around delayed deliveries and unclear delivery timelines, and defined them as the core design problem.
